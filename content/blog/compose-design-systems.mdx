## Why it matters

A shared design system keeps mobile UI coherent as the app scales.

## What I focus on

- Typography scale.
- Spacing tokens.
- Color roles.
- Accessible component states.

## Compose lesson

When your components are reusable and predictable, the product feels faster even before performance tuning.
