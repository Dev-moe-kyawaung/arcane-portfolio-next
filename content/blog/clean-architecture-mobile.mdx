## The problem

When mobile apps grow, tangled state and mixed responsibilities slow every release.

## The solution

Use explicit layers, stable contracts, and UI state that is easy to reason about.

## What matters most

- Clear boundaries.
- Simple data flow.
- Testable business logic.
- Reusable Compose UI patterns.

## My rule

A feature should be understandable from the top level without needing a mental debugger.
